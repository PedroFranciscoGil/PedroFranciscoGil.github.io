I currently work on stellarator optimization at Max-Planck-Institute for Plasma Physics, where I study how to improve the optimization of stellarator coils and plasma topology.

I completed my PhD at Ludwig Maximilian University Munich under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Hartmut Zohm, where I studied how to design a stellarator to confine electrons and positrons and advance fusion reactors. My research interests include nonlinear optimization, and the application of artificial intelligence and machine learning to complex optimization problems.

Research

My work translates physical problems into tractable optimization frameworks. I focus on reformulating ill-posed problems and navigating highly non-convex optimization landscapes.

Current main topic

Coil optimization for stellarators is non-trivial as there can be an infinite amount of coil shapes that reproduce the target magnetic field. Recent work where we applied an augmented Lagrangian formulation to the electromagnetic coil problem resulted in Pareto-optimized fusion configurations.

Other topics

Working on automation of stellarator coil design via an AI-driven framework that selects optimization strategies to find the best performing fusion reactor candidates.

Interests

  • Nonlinear and constrained optimization
  • Convex optimization and duality
  • Numerical linear algebra and large-scale solvers
  • Stochastic and robust optimization under uncertainty
  • Machine learning and AI for scientific design problems
  • Stellarator and fusion magnet design
